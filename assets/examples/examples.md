This file points to some examples that demonstrates the usage of this software. Click on the header to go to the file.

These files were exported from the mobile version, so you will have to manually change the beat sounds after loading for the desktop versions.

## [Normal Metronome](./normal.rhy)
Easiest example, works the same way as any other metronome. Just playing the pulse.

## [Fifteen](./15.rhy)
This is for practicing different rhythmic gourpings in the time signature of 15/16. The structure goes like this: 4 bars of 4443, 2 bars of 33333, 2 bars of 555, repeat.

## [Footprints](./footprints.rhy)
This maps Footprints by Terence Blanchard. The rhythm is pretty simple, the pulse is just the bpm, and the meter/time-signature is 4/4. The hypermeter is constructed of 8 pulses (or quarter notes), therefore 32 sixteenth notes in total. The second rhythm repeats the 4 3 (where the subdivision is 4), therefore adding up to 7, and this gets repeated for 4 times (4*7 = 28), and we fill the last 4 with a quarter note, then the whole pattern repeats.

## [Metric Mod Exercise](./mm.rhy)
This demonstrates how metric modulation can be practiced. This changes alternates between original BPM and BPM after metric modulation of 3/4 (or eighth-notes-triplets becomes the new sixteenth note). Track 1 repeats the pulse for 8 times before and after the modulation. Track 2 plays the subdivision (1/4 of the pulse, or sixteenth note), with a hint of the subdivision of the next BPM before the modulation.

## [La Danse Du Bonheur](./la_danse.rhy)

## [Arithmophobia](./arithmophobia.rhy)

## [Language I](./lang.rhy)
This maps *Language I: Intuition* by The Contortionists, specifically the the part from roughly 2:07 - 3:03. 

## [Sentinel](./the_sentinel.rhy)
This maps the first two parts of *The Sentinel* by Car Bomb, which is from beginning to 0:41. I used different subdivisions between the first and second part instead of the metric modulation item in this because I felt that this makes more sense.
